Support

Help with the Breathe app by BuddyLabs.

Contact Us

Email us at support@buddylabs.app. You can also reach us from the app under Settings → Contact Support. Please include your device model and app version, which you can find under Settings → Version.

Managing Your Subscription

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple or Google.

  • iPhone and iPad: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Android: open Google Play, tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. Cancelling takes effect at the end of that period.

Restoring Purchases

If you reinstalled the app or changed devices, open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same App Store or Google Play account you used to subscribe.

Deleting Your Account

Open Settings → Delete Account in the app. This immediately and permanently removes your account and all associated data. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription, so cancel it first as described above.

If you can no longer access the app, use the account deletion request form.

Advertising and Privacy Choices

Users of the free plan see ads; Premium subscribers do not. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you can change your advertising consent at any time from Settings → Privacy Choices.
